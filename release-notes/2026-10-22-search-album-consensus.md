Search results now use peer album-track consensus as part of smart ranking and can also be sorted directly by the consensus score. The score compares normalized audio track names, order, and available lengths across matching peers, ignores non-audio files, and appears only when at least two sources support a shared track list. The consensus badge shows the inferred track list.
